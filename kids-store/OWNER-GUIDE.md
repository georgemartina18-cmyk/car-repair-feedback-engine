# ABICEE KIDS: How to manage your website products 📱

Everything is done from your phone. No computer and no code needed.

## ➕ Add a new product
1. Open your **"ABICEE KIDS – Add product"** form (use the shortcut on your home screen).
2. Fill in:
   - **Product name**, e.g. *Yellow Sundress*
   - **Category**: Baby, Girls, Boys, Shoes, Accessories or Essentials
   - **Price** in GH₵, numbers only, e.g. *150*
   - **Old price**: only if it's on sale, e.g. *200*. The website shows it ~~crossed out~~.
   - **Sizes**: separate them with commas, e.g. *2-3 yrs, 4-5 yrs, 6-7 yrs*. Leave empty if it's one size.
   - **Badge** (optional): *New*, *Hot* or *Sale*
   - **Photo**: tap *Add file* and choose **up to 5 pictures** from your gallery. Customers can
     swipe through them and tap to see them full size.
   - **Colours** (optional): separate them with commas, e.g. *White, Black, Orange*. Customers
     pick a colour and it shows in their order.
   - **Shop code**, if you were given one
3. Tap **Submit**. ✅
4. The product appears on the website within about **5 minutes**.

## 🎨 Products with several colours
You have two options:
- **One photo per colour (best).** Upload the photos **in the same order** as you typed the
  colours. For colours *White, Black, Orange*, upload the white one first, then black, then
  orange. When a customer taps "Orange", the website shows the orange photo.
- **One photo showing all colours together.** Upload that single picture and type the colours.
  Customers still choose their colour.

Simple colour names like *White, Black, Red, Pink, Navy, Cream, Baby blue* show as coloured
dots. Other names, like *Ankara print*, show as words. Both work.

## ✏️ Change a price, name, sizes or colours
1. Open the **Google Sheets** app and tap the **"ABICEE KIDS – Add product (Responses)"** sheet.
2. Tap the cell you want to change, type the new value, and tap ✓.

## 🚫 Sold out or remove a product
In the Google Sheets app, find the product's row and scroll right to the **Status** column:

| Type this in Status | What happens on the website |
|---|---|
| *(leave empty)* | Shows normally |
| `Sold out` | Still shows, with a grey **Sold out** label; customers can't add it to the bag |
| `Hide` | Removed from the website (you can bring it back by clearing the cell) |

## 📸 Tips for great photos
- Use **daylight** near a window, not flash.
- Use a **plain light background**, such as a white bedsheet or a wall.
- **Square photos** look best, so crop to square in your gallery app.
- Keep the item centred and fill most of the picture.

## 📲 Put the form on your home screen (one time)
1. Open the form link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮** (top right), then **Add to Home screen**, then **Add**.

## 🛍️ When a customer orders
Customers can order in **two ways**:

1. **On the website.** You get an **email** titled *"🛍️ New order AK-XXXX"*, and the order
   appears in the **Orders** tab of your Google Sheet.
2. **On WhatsApp.** The order arrives as a WhatsApp message, the same as before.

**For every order:**
1. **Call or WhatsApp the customer** on the number they gave to confirm the item, size and
   delivery location.
2. For **Mobile Money**, wait until the payment shows in your MoMo **before sending** the parcel.
   Customers use the order number (e.g. *AK-7F3K2*) as the payment reference.
3. In the **Orders** tab, change **Status** from `New` to `Confirmed`, then `Paid`, then
   `Sent`, so you always know where each order is.
